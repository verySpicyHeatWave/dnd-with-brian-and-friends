### A New Friend?!

The party wakes in the camp that they set up outside of the Wizard's tower. They look back on the tower but find that the tower is gone entirely. Strange, but now knowing what the tower was, it makes sense. The party continues its journey to Squawhorn, to address the Imp slavery they suspect is happening at the Twilight Court under Aelyra's father's guidance.

Elowen feels the wind shift in a meaningful direction, off the path and into nature a little bit. Elowen, who's canonically on that wind shit, tells her adventuring friends that they should take this path off the path. Everyone else is a little bit skeptical, particularly Syren, but they trust Elowen and so they agree.

Elowen finds an injured, sad Capybara, and realizes this is where the wind was guiding her. Elowen uses her magic to heal the Capybara. In good health, the Capybara nuzzles against Elowen, and seems to want to stay with her. Vesper hears some rustling through the trees, and some footsteps. A Tiefling woman appears, with red skin, purple horns and hair.

Vesper's got her daggers drawn.

"Looks like youuuu knew what you were doin' there," the Tiefling woman says to Elowen.
 
Syren, drink in hand, says quietly to herself, "I like her."

"Yeah, I dabble in healing," Elowen responds.

"Where are you all heading to?" asks the Tiefling.

"To the Twilight Court, in Squawhorn."

Silence for a moment. Elowen and the Tiefling woman inspect each other. Elowen asks in Druidic, "Do you also know how to heal animals? I'onno..."

In perfect Druidic, the Tiefling says, "I do, it's very important to me."

More silence. The tension eases. Vesper puts her daggers away, but is still eyeing the Tiefling.

"I love it here," Syren says. She takes another drink and lays down in the grass. She starts making grass angels.

The [skeleton](../../npcs/undead/skeleton) clicks his bones and smiles.

"What brings you here?" says Elowen.

"I live nearby. I heard some rustling and came to check it out," she responds.

Aelyra sks, "So, uhh, do you want to come with us?"

"Woah woah wait a minute," Vesper says. "That's a little too forward."

"So you live in the woods?" asks Vesper.

"'Ya homeless or what?" asks Elowen.

"I just stay here, this is where I live," the Tiefling responds.

"Boooriing," Syren says as she mixes herself a Martini.

"Well, are you looking for an adventure?" asks Vesper.

"Well... Yeah, sure!" the Tiefling responds. She introduces herself as Caenwin. 

After some further introductions and familiarizing, they all continue on the path towards Squawhorn. An uneventful couple of days pass as they continue into Squawhorn and towards the Twilight Court.

### Entering The Twilight Court (featuring Edward and Jacob)

One day in mid morning, the castle is in sight. They'll reach it before the evening. They pass the beautiful country fields where the wheat is grown and, to their surprise, find no imp slaves, which was their entire impetus (haha) for coming here--to stop the slavery. This is curious, but they continue. 

The country fields slowly fade and the road becomes more cobblestone. They pass through small hamlets. They walk, and they just keep walking.

They find a bridge that they cross often, passes over a small river. There is a bridge troll who guards the bridge. The troll says, "wassap bitches, you're back!" And Aelyra says, "What's up, [Jacob](../../npcs/undead/jacob-the-bridge-troll)? How you doing?" And he says, "biiiiiitch, it's not going good." They engage in a little bit of small talk.

Syren buys drugs from Jacob. Everyone is agast! Syren says, "This is how he lets us across the bridge."

Vesper says, "This place is worse than Ratwhistle..." They continue past the bridge and approach the castle.

The castle is an enormous, beautifully-crafted white marble castle with a wrought iron gate barring entrance. As soon as the guards see Aelyra, they begin opening the gate and letting them into the city that surrounds the royal court. As they pass, a couple of guards approach Aelyra as they enter and one of them says to her, "Your father wants to see you, and all of these... people." Then the guard sees Syren and says, "DAMN Syren what's good bro?!" and they, of course, dap up as was customary for the time.

Syren takes out the drugs she got from Jacob the Bridge Troll and says to the guard, "I got this for you."

"Hell yeah", the guard says. "Tell Jacob I said what's up!"

The guards escort the entire adventuring party to the throne, where [King Caladwen](../../npcs/humanoid/emporer-caladwen) is waiting. He speaks to his daughter.

"There you are, daughter! Thank you for coming home and saving us the trouble."

"It seems like you've already gone through some trouble, Daddy."

"Ho-ho-ho, you don't even know the half of it."

"Yeah, I'm sure I don't. Gross."

Syren says, "I'm so sorry, your highness."

"Don't kiss my ass, Syren!"

Aelyra says, "Take those aviators off! Wait, fuck, put them back on! Get some visine!"

"When will you come to your senses? You are the heiress to my throne!"

Aelyra makes a gag sound.

"You have to take this seriously. You can't be running off on your '***adventures***' trying to '***help people***'. You're noble born! You can't be running around with this... What are you, a thief? Some weird wind hippie, and a literal demon thingy. And a skeleton? Shame on you."

"Don't forget the capybara," says Elowen.

"The capybara is cool," says the king.

"I don't know what to tell you, daddy-o," says Aleyra, "I don't vibe with your imp slavery, and your corruption, and your greed. And that's why I left, I couldn't take it anymore. Syren came with me to keep me safe, so I'm fine and you don't need to worry about me, but I'm not gonna stand by while you commit crimes."

"Did you say... *imp slavery*?" the king asks.

"...Huh? No, I didn't say that," says Aelyra.

"You said that, just now," says Vesper.

"SILENCE, THIEF!" says the king.

"Sorry, not all of us were born into money," says Vesper.

"And that's the problem, isn't it?" responds the king. He turns back to Aelyra. "You know what? I've had it with your games, I've had it with your rebellion, your disrespect for me and for the throne. Guards, take them *all* to the dungeons."

Syren speaks up. "Hang on. I'm one of your guards! I just went to protect her... Sir. I kept your heiress alive. Can I please not go to the dungeons?"

The king's face turns red. "If you were worth **half** a shit as your father, you would have kept her here instead of galavanting across Estharia!"

"I really don't want to go the dungeon. I've drank a lot, sir."

"She's really drunk," adds Aelyra.

"Then it'll be that much easier. Guards, take them to the dungeons. They are to be sentenced to death, for treason. For crimes against this country." The king turns to Aelyra. "Daughter, approach me."

She approaches, and he says, "You know too much. I can't, with all of your rebellion, let you leave this castle alive."

Aelyra looks around, panicked for a moment. A plan formulates in her head. A good plan. No, like, a really good plan. A great plan. Maybe the best plan. She resolves to carry out this plan, counting on her new friends to understand what her plans are.

She clears her throat and prepares herself, assured in the pure brilliance of her plan. It's foolproof, it can't possibly fail.

"BLINDNESS!" 

She casts a Blindness spell into the king's face, but the king is able to dispel it. "Guards! She has assaulted the king!"

The party is surrounded by guards. Vesper pulls out her bow, but Syren tells her, "bro, chill." They relent, deciding collectively that they will try to escape.

In one final attempt to plea, Aelyra says, "That was an accident, I wasn't actually trying to blind you, I just sneezed."

"Interesting," the king says, "that you sneezed the word 'blindness' directly into my face with both of your hands."

"I know, crazy, right?" says Aelyra.

### Drama in the Dungeon

Everyone except for the Capybara is brought to the dungeons, all in the same large jail cell. The king keeps the Capybara with him in the throne room, because everyone agrees that he is cool as fuck. Vesper is able to sneak a single dagger into the jail cell into her boot, but no one else is able to effectively conceal any of the items that they want to try and escape the dungeons.

The jail cell is dark and damp and moldy and cold. There is one other person in there, an old, insane, disheveled husk of a man chained to the wall. "Oh hello, ladies! Oh ha hah hogh hahad huimsm ha hdhehehe aho!"

"Disgusting," says Elowen. They all move as far away from that guy as possible.

"Well guys... Sorry my dad sucks, I tried to tell you," says Aelyra.

"Yeah, thanks a lot," says Syren, who is eating a breadstick.

"Where'd you get a breadstick?!" says Elowen.

The party looks around and they see that there is one guard at the dungeon entrance. He's far enough away that he can't hear them talk or see them. Vesper is searching the room, touching the walls, looking for ways out. They get brief memories of their deaths from in the tower as they feel around the cold stone walls.

Syren recognizes that the guard is her ex boyfriend, [Rowan Thorp](../../npcs/humanoid/rowan-thorp) and she thinks she might be able to woo him with her wily charms.

"Hey Thorp--I mean, Rowan," she says.

"What do you want, *Syren*?"

"How have you been?"

"Fine."

"Yeah?"

"Yeah."

Some silence passes. The rest of the party watches on awkwardly.

"You fuckin' cheated on me," Rowan says.

"Ohhhhhhhhhh!" everyone yells.

"I did not!" says Syren.

"You did!"

"When?"

"With other Thorp! You cheated on me with Thorp Rowan!"

"Isn't that you?" asks Vesper.

"No, I'm Rowan Thorp! It was fucked up, honestly. We don't look alike!

"I don't know who told you this, but they are full of shit. I am yours and only yours."

"We broke up so long ago, and honestly I'm over it. It's been like eight months!" He smells. "Dude! Are you drunk? I can smell it!"

"...Would you like a drink?"

"Ohh my fucking god, not this again. I can't do this again!" Vesper and Elowen reach out to try and console Rowan.

"This relationship has me so traumatized, you guys. Who even are you?"

"Hi, Vesper," says Vesper, "Um, she's gonna bug you about this the whole time in here. You should just let us out of here!"

"No way, I would be killed," he says.

"What if you come with us?" says Syren.

"Look, things are going really well for me right now. I have a new girlfriend and she doesn't drink or anything."

"What's her name?" demands Syren.

"Bella."

Aelyra says, "Ugh, she's terrible. She's the bitch who tried to outrun you at the running contest."

"Yeah," says Syren, "She cheated. She threw a stick in front of me. It was a big stick."

"Yeah well you ALSO cheated! With Thorp Rowan!"

"Come on, Thorp, you're killing me, man," says Aelyra.

"It's Rowan," he corrects. "Thorp and her totally ruined my life. I was depressed."

"Awwww," says everyne except Syren.

"Things are going good for me right now! I'm not trying to lose my job and my life." 

"She's the heiress! When the king dies, do you wanna be on HER shitlist?" 

"She hands tomorrow, why do I care?"

"Hey, buddy," says Vesper, "are you gonna propose to Caitlyn?"

"I don't know, guys, we haven't been together that long, but I do really like--WHY AM I EVEN TALKING TO YOU? I'm leaving!"

"Can we convince you to let us out?" asks Syren.

"Not a chance." And he walks away.

"Rowan, how much do you make? Slightly above minimum wage? Imagine this. We want to overthrow the king. This is is heiress, as you know, and you're like, 'oh she's gonna hang tomorrow', but here's the deal: She won't, if you let us out. And you wanna know what happens to you if you let her out? Captain of the guard. Captain Rowan Thorp, imagine it. No more dungeon duty."

"Wait, hold on," he says. "You guys are gonna overthrow the king?"

"No, no," says Elowen.

"Vesper is gonna kill the king for us," says Syren.

Vesper looks around nervously.

"You guys are gonna overthrow the king? You remember why you're in here, right?"

"...I rolled a bad number."

"For treason! And crimes against your country!"

Aelyra can't take this anymore. She prepares a Suggestion spell and casts it on Rowan. She says to him, "You will let us out of the cell, give us our stuff back, and help us escape the dungeon. *sha na na na*," she says.

Rowan blinks and stares for just a second, and then says, "Okay, no problem." He unlocks the cell.

"YEAH! That's my best friend!" says Syren.

"Thanks, Rowan," says Vesper.

"You're welcome, of course!" says Rowan.

The whole party as well as the creepy old man start leaving the cell. The old man says, "YEaeeeahhh ladiesssss heh hyeh hsh hhaaj hd heh hou hui."

"Ew, put him back, put him back," says Elowen.

Rowan escorts them through the dungeon, past some other guards. When questioned, he says, "No, it's no problem, I'm just letting them out of the dungeon." The guards are stupefied, but believe that it must be important. Vesper steals Rowan's keys. He gets the party to the entrance to the dungeon, he opens the chest that holds their things and lets them get their things back. Then he opens the door and says, "Okay, go ahead."

They all sprint away. The old man runs out in some other direction, who knows where he went.
