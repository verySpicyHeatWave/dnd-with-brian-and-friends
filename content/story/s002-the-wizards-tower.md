## Highlights

- The party wakes up in Norville the morning after the Lamp Emporium incident, feeling stronger and wiser.
- Elowen communes with the wind and receives a vision: enslaved imps in chains, laboring under the sun near the elven castle of Squawhorn, and dozens of demonic contracts with the signature **Gwarveaux**.
- Aelyra comes downstairs to find a letter waiting for her, sealed with the crest of the Twilight Court. Her father knows she's gone.
- The party, motivated by the vision and by Aelyra's need to put some distance between herself and whoever her father sends after her, agrees to head to Squawhorn and free the enslaved imps.
- On the road, Vesper finds a buyer for part of Yuck's tail, trading it in exchange for having her dagger and a new amulet imbued with the imp's acidic essence.
- The party stumbles across a wizard's tower that definitely wasn't there when they passed this way before. With a storm rolling in, they decide to spend the night inside.
- The tower is a time loop. The party dies a lot. It's fine.
- On their way through the tower, they encounter giant rats, mud mephits with sticky fingers, a talking riddle door, a magical mirror that does unsettling things, and a Nothic who has been trapped in the tower so long that it has warped and twisted him into something unrecognizable.
- The party fights the Nothic and wins, then destroys the magical orb at the top of the tower, freeing a skeleton who's been trapped inside cleaning the same room for who knows how long, and escaping into the sunlight.

## Story

### Morning in Norville

The morning after the Lamp Emporium, the sun is rising and the Lute & Lyre is quiet. The smell of beer and breakfast rise up through the floor boards and into the inn chambers. Birds sing and chirp outside, deaf to the hellish events of the night before.

[Elowen](../../main-characters/elowen-giggleleaf) is the first to stir. She opens the window of her little room and lets the wind in, closing her eyes as it washes over her. But this morning the wind has something to say. The air grows hot and strange — hotter than any spring morning has any business being — and it encircles her, faster and faster, like a growing whirlwind.

In the window of her mind, she can see the wind carrying with it dozens and dozens of pages, swirling around her. She reaches out in her mind and snatches one from the air. The page is covered in text, written in some foul demonic language that she cannot comprehend. It appears to be a contract. At the bottom of the page, in the common tongue, is a single signature which reads the name **Gwarveaux**.

Looking up, past the swirling of pages, she looks to the sun setting on the horizon. Hundreds or thousands of imps in chains, tilling fields and hauling carts, the silhouette of the elven castle of [Squawhorn](../../setting/estharia/squawhorn) looming behind them.

As the wind calms and cools, the image fades from her mind but the memory remains. She opens her eyes and goes down to the dining hall.

[Aelyra](../../main-characters/aelyra-caladwen) comes downstairs for breakfast and is informed by the morning bartender that a letter arrived for her. After the bartender spends an embarrassing amount of time failing to locate said letter, Aelyra finds it herself. It was sitting in plain sight, sealed with the unmistakable crest of the [Twilight Court](../../setting/estharia/the-twilight-court). The letter is brief, and reads:

> Dearest Alira,<br>
> We know that you have exited our land, and we are coming for you.<br>
> From,<br>
> Your Lord Father<br>
> P.S. Don't trust everyone you meet.

Aelyra sighs, folds the envelope and puts it away. Out in the tavern she spots Elowen sitting at the same table that she silently observed from the night prior, before the events at [the lamp emporium](../../story/s001-the-lamp-shop.md). She takes a seat next to Elowen.

[Vesper](../../main-characters/vesper-dreadway) wakes up thinking, inexplicably, about [Syren](../../main-characters/syren-ashryver). There's something about her — something Vesper can't quite name, a familiarity that she can't account for. She lies in bed turning it over for a moment before Marzipan quacks her back to reality, and she gets up and heads downstairs to join the others.

Syren comes downstairs last, looking especially in rough shape. Sunglasses hide her bloodshot eyes, a half-empty water bottle barely held in her loose grasp. She stops by the front desk, requests a couple of aspirin, and throws them back as she slowly walks towards the table with the rest of the party.

The four of them end up at a table together. "I hope they have pancakes," says Aelyra.

They do.

As the party eats, Aelyra shares the letter and discusses the contents. Elowen recognizes the seal of the Twilight Court, which reminds her of the vision, and so she shares the details of that. Imps in chains at Squawhorn, where Aelyra's father resides,

"What's in it for me?" asks Vesper. Classic rogue.

Aelyra lists all of the treasures available in the Twilight Court, including magical lamps and daggers. She promises Vesper a ruby-encrusted magical dagger named Delilah from her father's treasury, and Elowen expresses interest in a magical lamp.

The group agrees: they're going to Squawhorn.

### The Market

Before leaving Norville, the party swings by the market for supplies — rations, bedrolls, the basics for a few days of road travel. It's while browsing that the imp named [Yuck's](../../npcs/imps/yuck.md) tail, draped around Vesper's neck, catches the eye of a strange masked merchant at a nondescript and seemingly empty stand.

"Excuse me, young miss, but where'd you get that?" he asks.

The merchant turns out to know his infernal materials. He identifies the tail by its color and smell as belonging to an imp of an acidic, poisonous nature, and offers to work it into something useful. He knows [Ratwhistle](../../setting/estharia/ratwhistle). He knows Vesper's father, Thad, and they bond over Thad Miller.

She walks away with one of her daggers imbued with the tail's acidic properties — it now deals extra acid damage on a hit and has taken on a distinctly pinkish hue — and a matching amulet that provides resistance against poison and acid once per day. The merchant keeps what's left of the tail. Everyone is happy.

The party loads up and heads out of Norville, bound eastward for Squawhorn.

### Where Did That Tower Come From?

The road to Squawhorn runs roughly four days on foot. The party makes good time on the first day, moving through open country and farmland. Sometime in the late afternoon of the second day, with a storm building dark and fast on the horizon, they come around a bend in the road and see a tower in the distance.

Tall, narrow, slightly crooked, with moss growing up one side and vines beginning to reclaim the lower stones. The towering tower towers over them, toweringly. It looks completely abandoned. Neither Syren nor Aelyra recall seeing this tower on this road when they just passed through mere days prior. It is strange.

Elowen pays close attention to the storm, looking for signs as to whether or how they can weather it. She concludes that camping in it could be very dangerous. The tower is right there. It's not doing anything. It's just standing there being a tower, almost inviting them in, offering the comfort and safety that they need.

They go inside.

The inside of the tower is exactly what the outside promised: dusty, dim, cobwebbed, full of broken crates that have been empty long enough to start rotting. There is a single door across the room, and above it, one torch burning on the wall. No footprints in the dust. The only entrance is the door they came in through.

Vesper presses her ear to the door and hears nothing. She opens it and finds a climbing stairwell. They go up.

The second floor is more of the same — dark, quiet, abandoned, the kind of place that suggests no one has been here in a very long time but something still feels subtly wrong about the texture of the dust and the wear on the walls. The party investigates rigorously, meticulously. They all come to the same conclusion: the age of this place doesn't feel natural. Like something is maintaining the aesthetic of abandonment without fully committing to it.

Without anything actionable, and instead just the vague notion of inauthenticity, they keep moving towards the door across the room, and up another stairwell.

This floor is different: instead of a large, circular room, it's more of a long hallway. One wall, floor to ceiling, is entirely occupied by a single enormous mirror, and another unassuming door lies in the wall at the far end. Similarly to the room below, something about this mirror gives them a vague sense of dread. They inspect the mirror, but nothing seems obvious.

As they pass, Aelyra looks in the mirror. For a brief moment, it appears that her reflection blinks when she does not. She stares. It doesn't do it again, but something is definitely wrong with this mirror.

Again, they go through the door and up another stairwell.

The fourth floor is not unoccupied. A family of giant rats who have set up a nest in a corner of the room and are deeply uninterested in sharing the space. The characters walk past the rats and straight towards the next door, but this time find it locked. After a lot of searching, the key to the locked door is found... lying in the rat's nest.

Elowen attempts to befriend them with her animal friendship spell. The rats, however, are not interested in friendship. They're interested in maintaining their personal space. COVID rules and all that, please.

The party retreats to the staircase to regroup and brainstorm some ideas. Aelyra decides that she will just retrieve the key with her Mage Hand spell. The Mage Hand reaches into the nest, takes the key, and retreats with the rats none the wiser. The lock opens. They go through, and up more stairs.

The door to the fifth floor has a large serene-looking face, eyes and mouth closed, carved into the wood. As the party approaches, the eyes open and the mouth says, in the bored tone of someone who has been doing this for a very long time:

"Are you looking to enter?"

They are.

"I will only let you in if you answer my riddle."

He presents his riddle: *What must be broken before it can be used?*

Vesper knows this one. "An egg!"

"Correct!" The face opens the door. They go through. They go up the stairs. They come out on the first floor, back where they came in. Across the room, a second torch is now lit on the wall, beside the first.

### What the Fuck is Going On?

"No way," Vesper says.

The door they just came through seems to have circled right back to the beginning. But, how is that possible? Aelyra tries to take the torches off the wall, but they're firmly affixed. She knocks on the door twice. But there is no answer, only the silence of time passing by.

"Well," says Aelyra. And they go back up, clearly growing frustrated with this place.

As they enter the familiar second floor, the door behind them closes, and the room begins to fill with a noxious gas. Very quickly, their vision is obscured and maintaining a sense of direction becomes difficult as the gas disorients them mentally. Aelyra, Syren, and Elowen all make it to the door in time, but Vesper becomes lost in the fog. As her vision dims, she calls out for help. Elowen hears her calls, takes in a deep breath, and sprints back into the room in the direction of the calls. She finds Vesper and sprints back towards the exit. Everyone is safe, and up the stairs they go.

The third floor once again has the long mirror, but opposite the mirror are several portraits that were not there before. Each portrait is a near identical portrait of Vesper, but as they go further and further into the room, each portrait has a smile that grows larger and larger and larger, until at the end the smile is almost too large for her face.

Vesper draws one of her daggers and slashes the first portrait, to no apparent effect except to reveal the brick wall behind the canvas. She approaches the next one and starts slapping it and tries to remove it from the wall. But nothing happens. They turn towards the mirror, looking for any sign of something happening, but nothing. They all look pretty good in this mirror, actually. Nice.

Vesper becomes enraged. They only came into this stupid tower to rest through a storm, and now they're being tormented. One by one, Vesper violently slashes all of the paintings, and at the end, when they are all slashed and nothing has happened, they sigh and move on up the stairs.

As they enter the fourth floor, it is this time completely covered with mud, Piles of it all over the room, the walls and ceiling are essentially coated with it. Strange, they think, and as they wade through, Mud Mephits arise from the mud. The foremost mephit gestures towards Aelyra with its hands outstretch and palms open. Aelyra recognizes the gesture and lies, "Oh but I only have six gold pieces!"

The party gathers to discuss the amount of gold that they have. Aelyra turns back and says, "How much gold do you want, little guy?"

*"I wants the moneys and treasures!"* it responds.

"Oh, well how much money and treasures do you want?"

*"As much as you has!"*

"Well, I have six whole pieces for you right here. Do you want six of them?"

*"...I thinks you has more..."*

"I have a fake mustache. You want that?"

The Mephits begin to rabble a little bit, speaking over one another, negotiating the demands.

From behind Aelyra, a Mephit sniffs around her pack and announces, *"I smells a demon on this one."*

Aelyra calls out, "Let me see the key, please!" The Mephits all stop talking. One of them in the back, a smaller one, holds up the key which is almost too big for his hands and calls out, *"I has the key!"* Everyone is enamored with that little guy. What a freaking cutie.

Aelyra calls out, "Okay well I have six gold pieces and this little horn," and she produces from her pack [Belvedere's](../../npcs/demons/belvedere) horn.

A mephit turns to Elowen. *"What about you?"* She replies, "I also have six gold pieces, and I will give them to you. Also I have a lamp."

*"No! I think I need more treasure."*

"But it's a really nice lamp. It's magical. Imps made it."

*"Let me see inside your pack."*

"See inside my pack? I can't do that. There's tampon in there, I have feminine products. You perv!"

The mephits turn to Vesper and give her the same interrogation.

"I have *seven* gold pieces!" And she produces six gold pieces and one of her chocolate coins. "This one is a human delicacy, it's great. Try it!"

The mephit takes the coin and bites right into it, through the foil and all. Everyone winces as they think about biting into that thing with a filling or something, ugh, that sucks to even think about. Even one of the other mephits cringes a little bit.

*"That's okay...* the Mephit says.

"I have more of those! I have a lot more!"

Syren, through her sunglasses, takes a sip of the coffee and rubs her throbbing head. She watches through her hangover the mephits confer with one another, discussing. Several mephits sink into the mud, and the foremost mephit remains. "That is a deal, we will takes your golds and your chocolates and your lamps. We don't wants no demon horns though, too dangerous."

The small mephit arises from the mud and hands the key to Syren while everyone else hands over the meager amounts of gold and trinkets that they offered, completely unaware of the mephits rooting around in their packs behind them, stealing all of the gold from within.

The lead mephit laughs, thanks the party kindly, and sinks into the mud with the others.

The players travel up to the next floor. The door greets them again, remembering them, and poses another riddle: "I have a neck but no head, and I wear a cap but have no hair. What am I?" Aelyra knows this one. "A bottle!" The door opens, and they climb the stairs...

...to find themselves back at the entrance again. Now, a third torch is lit above the door.

### Okay, This is Getting Ridiculous

Elowen sighs. "Okay, burn the wizard's tower down." The players turn towards the door that they just came from--it's the front door, the main entrance that they initially took to get in. They consider just leaving the tower, here and now, and abandoning this place. But they decide against it. They decide to press on ahead.

Elowen approaches the door and knocks three time, again to no response. They open the door. More fucking stairs.

As they enter the second floor, once again the door behind them locks, only this time the ceiling begins to lower on them. Panicked, they all race towards the door. Vesper dashes expertly towards the end, but the others just don't have the drive to do it. As the ceiling lowers and they have less room to run, they're pushed down into the floor. The ceiling presses on them, squeezes them. Everything goes black.

They come to back where they just were, at the first floor, with the three torches lit before them. They all touch their bodies, vividly remembering their deaths from just seconds ago. "What the hell?"

They compose themselves, and Aelyra gets an idea to construct some sort of "situation" with the debris that litters the first floor to keep the ceiling from coming down. Again, they climb up the stairs and rush into the room. Aelyra quickly places her situation and dashes towards the door. This time, it's Vesper who doesn't quite make it in time, and is crushed by the ceiling.

Back to the entrance.

Okay, one last attempt: Aelyra and the party really put their best foot forward in constructing the situation. It's well built. Again, they go up the stairs. Again, they rush in and emplace the situation, and this time, the party makes it through all together, and they ascend once more into the room with the mirror room.

Now, as they enter, written in large letters across the entire mirror is the name Aelyra, in dripping warm blood. Vesper places a single hand on the mirror and asks, "Mirror mirror on the wall, who's the fairest of them all?" and her reflection smiles and winks at her. She then draws her dagger, enchanted with magic from the imp's tail, and stabs her own reflection.

As soon as the dagger makes contact with the mirror, a pink hue spreads across the mirror, freezing the reflections in place. This gives Aelyra an idea, and she draws from her pack Belvedere's horn and stabs it in the same place where Vesper's dagger stabbed. From there, the mirror turns black, which also spreads out from that point. As the darkness spreads across the plane, disembodied whispering voices fill the room.

Aelyra casts Vicious Mockery, absolutely roasting the disembodied voices around them. She says, "You fucking suck man. I don't want to do this anymore, the stairs suck as hard as you do."

A whisper from the cacophony responds, "We are trapped here too."

The party gasps. Aelyra questions whether or not absolutely roasting the shit out of other victims of this tower was a good idea when again, a singular voice pierces through the noise with a warning.

"They replaced us. Don't let them replace you."

The party stands silently for a little bit before moving on to the next floor, absorbing the warning they just receive.

Once they arrive on the fourth floor, the room is scorched. Soot and burn marks everywhere. Three Magmin are sitting quietly in a corner. When they notice the party enter, they immediately spring into action. "We want something to burn! We want something to burn! Give us something to burn! Give us something to burn!"

Aelyra says, "Can you burn the mirror because that shit's annoying."

"Fuck that thing!"

Vesper says, "Yes. Go freaking melt it with your magma hand."

"It doesn't work! It won't burn!"

Elowen says, "Burn the house down."

"I need to burn something!"

Vesper offers the torch she's holding, realizing that it's not necessary. THe Magmin takes it in his hands and it immediately burns to ash. "That's not enough! I need more! Something to burn!"

All of the players hand over their torches. They have dark vision. Why did they even have these torches, like honestly? Kind of funny. But the torches don't sate the little Magmin, who are once again bouncing off the walls. "Please! More to burn!"

Vesper says, "Okay, we'll get you something. Wait for us, we'll be back." And the players back track all the way down to the first floor, where they planned to get some wood. But as they make their way down, Vesper realizes how frustrated she is with all of this, and when they get there, she immediately heads for the front door. "Fuck this, I'm done," she says, and she tries to open this front door, but finds it won't give way.

"Of course it's locked," she says, dejected.

Aelyra and Elowen, at the same time, both say, "What if they burn the door?"

Aelyra says, "Yes, bring them down and burn this door."

"No," Elowen says. "Send them up to burn the face door. I'm done with his riddles. I'm done with being teleported. Either I'm going to anamorph if I have to, or he's going to get burned."

While the two discuss what they want to do, Vesper gives it some thought. She thinks of all the creatures they've seen so far. The family of giant Rats. The Mephits, who seemed harmless overall. The trapped souls whispering from within the glass mirror. The front door feels too easy, there must be some reward at the end of all this.

"Let's burn the face-ass bitch," says Vesper.

The party returns to the Magmin with some wood, which again burns too quickly. The players tell the Magmin about the door upstairs, to which one says, "Door? Why didn't I think of door?" And they burn their way up the stairs into the next room.

The face door greets the players heartily. "Ha ha! Welcome back! I see you are ready for another--what are you doing? Oh! Oh! Oh! Noooo!" And the face door burns, gone completely. Tired and satisfied, the Magmin sit back down to rest, and fall asleep.

The players climb the stairs once more.

### The Nothic

On the fifth loop, the fourth floor has a new occupant. A [Nothic](../../npcs/other/the-nothic) — a misshapen, single-eyed aberration — is at the far end of the room, clawing desperately at the door, trying to get through it. He doesn't notice the party at first. He is entirely consumed with the door.

When he does notice them, he stares at each of them in turn with his one enormous eye, and he says things. Personal things. Things he could not possibly know. Then he tells them the knowledge beyond that door is his, only his, and they can't have it.

The DM explains what a Nothic is: a wizard or scholar, consumed so completely by his hunger for knowledge that it warped him, body and mind, into something else entirely. This creature came to the tower seeking the knowledge of the wizard who once lived here. He has been trying to get through that door ever since. For how long, it's impossible to say.

This doesn't make the party like him more, but it does make them understand him, which is something.

Combat begins. Aelyra opens with a vicious magical insult that deals psychic damage. The Nothic screams at her to stop. She does not stop. Vesper closes the distance in a single movement and drives the acid dagger into his shoulder. Elowen launches a bolt of radiant starlight that illuminates him from within and prevents him from going invisible.

Vesper, with the Nothic's attention fully on her, announces loudly that she thinks the door behind him is opening. The Nothic whips around. It is not opening. Vesper stabs him again while he's looking. Aelyra sees her opening, runs in, and buries her rapier in his neck.

The Nothic falls. His one eye rolls up. The room is quiet.

### The Wizard's Study

The riddlemaster lets them through without comment. One more flight of stairs. One more door.

The room at the top of the tower is clean and bright and smells faintly of old books and something else — something charged, the way the air smells before a storm. Shelves of leather-bound tomes line the walls. At a desk nearby lie scattered pages covered in arcane notation — the work of a time wizard, clearly, though the specifics are beyond any of them. There is a chest. There is a skeleton, quietly sweeping the floor, who does not look up.

And in the center of the room, low to the ground, large enough that you'd have trouble lifting it alone, glowing sky-blue and soft: the orb.

Aelyra looks into it. For a moment she can see everything — every version of the tower, every iteration of every room, every person who ever walked in here and didn't walk back out. She can see the Nothic when he first arrived: just a man, a scholar, eager to learn. She sees people taken by the mirror. She sees loops no one ever completed. She sees the whole terrible history of this place all at once, compressed into a single moment, and then she steps back and doesn't touch it again.

Elowen asks the skeleton if he's all right. He points at the orb.

The chest has 200 gold inside and a torn magical bag that is definitely a Bag of Holding and will be very useful once someone repairs the rip in it. There are spell scrolls. There is a spellbook. There are candelabras that Vesper immediately begins pocketing. Elowen takes the spellbook.

Then they smash the orb.

All four of them lift it together and let it drop and it shatters across the floor in a cascade of blue light. The skeleton stops sweeping. He holds very still for a moment. Then he looks at his hands. Then he looks at the party. Then he begins to dance — the unrestrained, uncomplicated dancing of something that has been waiting a very, very long time to be allowed to move freely.

The stairs have become slides. Nobody questions this.

On the way down, every floor is quiet and empty and genuinely abandoned. The rats are gone. The mephits are gone. The mirror room is just a room with a wall in it. When they pass through the entry hall, five torches burn on the wall, and the front door swings open to evening air and a clear sky and the smell of rain on the road.

The storm has passed. Squawhorn is still out there. They set up camp and rest.
