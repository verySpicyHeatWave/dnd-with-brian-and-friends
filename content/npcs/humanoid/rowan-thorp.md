Rowan Thorp is a guard of the Twilight Court who works dungeon duty. He is also [Syren's](../../../main-characters/syren-ashryver) ex. They had a really messy relationship that kind of messed him up, and left him depressed for a while. It is alleged that Syren cheated on him with Thorp Rowan, another person in the city who honestly looks nothing like Rowan Thorp.

After some therapy, Rowan found a new girlfriend name Caitlyn and it seems like things are going pretty good for him, and he doesn't want to screw that up.

During [Session #3](../../../../story/s003-the-twilight-throne), [Aelyra](../../../main-characters/aelyra-caladwen) casts Suggestion on him and forces him to release them from jail, despite his protestations.
