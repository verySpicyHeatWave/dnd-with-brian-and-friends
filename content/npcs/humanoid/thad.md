Thaddeus Miller (known as Thad) is the father of [Vesper Dreadway](../../../main-characters/vesper-dreadway). Described by many as a good man, Vesper would call him "the sweetest human you've ever met," and a man at market in [Norville](../../setting/estharia/norville) called him "salt of the earth." He gave Vesper her first dagger. 

He is alive and well, still living in [Ratwhistle](../../setting/estharia/ratwhistle).
