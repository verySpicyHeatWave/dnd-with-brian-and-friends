This is the Elven mother of [Syren](../../../main-characters/syren-ashryver) and [Vesper](../../../main-characters/vesper-dreadway). 

All we know of her is that she birthed Syren, but abandoned her early in Syren's life, running off somewhere. She ended up in Ratwhistle, fell in love with [Thad](../thad), and gave birth to Vesper. She died during childbirth.
