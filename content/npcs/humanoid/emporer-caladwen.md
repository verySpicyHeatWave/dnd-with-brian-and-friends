King Edward Caladwen is the King of Squawhorn, who sits the throne of The Twilight Court. He is the father to [Aelrya Caladwen](../../../main-characters/aelyra-caladwen).

The King is a greedy authoritarian, embezzling from or overtaxing commoners and running a fascist regime. When his daughter began publicly speaking out against him, he attempted to silence her and to force her obedience. When she disappeared, he became beset on bringing her home and molding her into the future Empress of the Twilight Court that she was born to be, whether she likes it or not.
