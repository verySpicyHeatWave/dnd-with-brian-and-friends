Skeleman is a friendly skeleton that was trapped in the study of [The Abandoned Wizard's Tower](../../../../story/s002-the-wizards-tower), forced to keep the study as a servant to whatever wizard once lived there. But when the tower was abandoned, the magic that worked in that place was never released, and so it worked and worked.

It was freed by the players and it said it wanted to join them, so it'll be following along.
