Jacob the Bridge Troll is a half-troll who lives in Squawhorn. He guards the bridge that leads up to the Castle. The only way to get past the bridge is to buy drugs from Jacob. He's pretty chill, even if he is also involved in the morally dubious businesses of bridge guarding and drug dealing. He is also Syren's gay neighbor, and he and his husband are looking to adopt a child soon.
